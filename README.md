## 🌐 Client work: websites delivered end to end

I've designed, built and now look after the online presence of two NDIS disability support providers. Having also worked in IT support at a disability organisation, I understand how much these businesses rely on technology that's simple, accessible and trustworthy, for both their staff and the people they support.

For both clients I handled everything from the first logo sketch to the last DNS record: branding, design, build, hosting, domains and business email.

### [Emuability](https://www.emuability.com.au): registered NDIS provider, NSW
- **Branding & design:** created the logo, visual identity and every graphic used on the site
- **Serverless AWS build**, hosted in the Sydney region (ap-southeast-2):
  - **S3 + CloudFront** for fast, reliable hosting and delivery
  - **AWS Certificate Manager (ACM)** for HTTPS across the site
  - **Lambda** to process referral, booking, feedback and contact form submissions
  - **DynamoDB** to store form submissions securely
  - **Amazon SES** to send email notifications when a new form comes in
  - **Cognito** for secure authentication
  - **IAM** roles and policies so each service only has the permissions it needs
- **Accessibility built in:** adjustable text size, high-contrast mode and reduced motion, plus an accessibility statement and privacy policy
- **Domain, DNS & email:** managed on GoDaddy, with business email set up through Microsoft 365 admin

### [MercyLight](https://www.mercylight.com.au): NDIS disability services provider
- **Branding & design:** logo, visual design and all site assets
- **Build:** Squarespace, so the team can update content themselves without needing a developer
- **Domain, DNS & email:** managed on GoDaddy, with business email set up through Microsoft 365 admin

## 🛠 Tools I've built

### [Clean Link Copier](https://github.com/vipercipher/clean-link-copier)
A Chrome extension that removes tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid and others) from links before you share them. Every day, people pass on links that quietly carry tracking data about where they came from. This fixes that in one click. It runs entirely in your browser and collects no data.
